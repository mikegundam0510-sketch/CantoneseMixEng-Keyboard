# Search-prefix ranking weights

`prefix_ranker.b64` contains bounded short Han, endpoint and remaining-code hint
weights from2,529 previously attributed training cases and552 project-authored
daily variants. Prefix rivals and actual baseline score margins provide the
pairwise training objective. Complete sentences are not model features.

HKCanCor training-conversation contributions: Luke, K. K., & Wong, M. L. Y. (2015),
The Hong Kong Cantonese Corpus, https://github.com/fcbond/hkcancor , CC BY4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Rime Cantonese words and the
previous attributed decoder contribute existing matching/scoring evidence;
their existing notices remain applicable.

Tatoeba Cantonese training contributions: Tatoeba contributors,
https://tatoeba.org/ , CC BY2.0 France
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). The existing bundled Tatoeba
notice preserves the pinned Jyut Dictionary release, selected source and license.
Project-authored template variants use synthetic priorities, not observed usage.
This fitted model is a modification; original authors do not endorse it.

words.hk dictionary examples with the Non-Commercial Open Data License1.0 are
evaluation-only development material. They never train this asset or enter the
APK. The separately attributed public-domain words.hk frequency exports retain
their original scope. No private user messages or runtime editor text are stored
in this immutable model. Phone personalization remains on the device.

Parameters, source/pair checksums, independent evaluation and selected asset
checksum are in `PREFIX_RANKER_REPORT.json`. This model shows one additional
top-five hit and no first-choice gain on800 dictionary usage examples. These
are not measured current Hong Kong chat frequency or physical Fold7 results.
