Only rows linked to that database's Tatoeba Cantonese source were used.
Other dictionary examples and the original database are not bundled.
Original contributor usernames are unavailable in the redistribution.

This project normalized, filtered and grouped the examples, and derived
bounded character n-gram counts from 3,703 training rows / 3,529 families.
Each family contributes each gram once, with weight 2 in the combined model.
Validation and heldout examples do not contribute training counts.
The distributor may already have converted simplified text to traditional.
The model retains existing HKCanCor, Rime and authored contributions under
their respective notices. The model does not contain a sentence database.
The generated vocabulary accompanying the trial was not installed.

These community examples are not measured Hong Kong chat frequency.
Neither Tatoeba nor Jyut Dictionary endorses this app or its modifications.
Source checksums and the model checksum are in SOURCE.json.
