# Conservative tail supplement research

The direct-code weights are a deterministic binary export of the frozen
`tools/data/code_generator/models/teacher.npz`, without refitting or new text.
The training data and model derivation remain those documented in
[`../code_generator/NOTICE.md`](../code_generator/NOTICE.md) and
[`../sentence_pretrain/NOTICE.md`](../sentence_pretrain/NOTICE.md).

2023 snapshot, CC BY 2.0 France; UD Cantonese: Wong, Gerdes, Leung and Lee,
CC BY-SA 4.0; Mozilla Common Voice Sentence Collector prompts, CC0; project
authored examples. These prompts are not a collection of modern private chat.
Source URLs, notices and hashes are preserved in the predecessor source audit.
No phone text or personal learning record has been collected.

Evaluation outputs retain the source terms of their evaluation cases.
They are not additional training material. The original Rime/Jackchow
dictionary notices remain unchanged. The full Common Voice prompt cache
is local and ignored; it is not republished here.
