# Evaluation sources

These100 validation and500 reserved examples are public, reviewed words.hk
dictionary usage, under Hong Kong Lexicography Limited's Non-Commercial Open
Data License1.0. They are evaluation-only, never training or APK assets.
The full license and attribution are retained in
[LICENSE-EXAMPLES.txt](../daily_prefix/LICENSE-EXAMPLES.txt) and
[NOTICE.txt](../daily_prefix/NOTICE.txt).

Previously observed families and tracked6+ target substrings, plus pinned raw
HKCanCor/UD/Tatoeba training exposure, are excluded before native decoding.
These dictionary examples do not measure current private Hong Kong typing or
physical Fold7 performance. No private conversations are collected.
