# Joint ranking data and models

Additional natural text comes only from the original 46 training conversations
of HKCanCor (Luke and Wong, 2015; CC BY 4.0) and pinned Tatoeba training groups
(Tatoeba contributors, via Jyut Dictionary; CC BY 2.0 France). These utterances
are new ranking examples, not newly collected messages or measured typing
frequency. Conversation/translation families retain their original identities.

The existing 5,381 attributed rows also include project-authored synthetic
scenarios and UD_Cantonese-HK (Wong, Gerdes, Leung and Lee, 2017; revision
68c3151c1181a7b67ef5c917570635bcccf7e4bf; CC BY-SA 4.0).
Derived fitted weights and encoded models are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.

[phrase relation sources](../phrase_relations/NOTICE.md),
[relation license](../../../app/src/main/assets/licenses/relations/README.md)
and [CC BY-SA 4.0](../../../app/src/main/assets/licenses/relations/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).

words.hk dictionary examples, including the new validation/reserve samples,
are Hong Kong Lexicography Limited NCDOL 1.0 evaluation-only material. They
never train a model or enter APK assets. The full attribution/data license
remain in [the dictionary notice](../daily_prefix/NOTICE.txt).
No private chats or phone learning records are collected or used for training.
