This project selected public/OK-reviewed entries, removed pronunciation and
markup, retained NFC glyphs and omitted internal commas for Han composition.
Headword, duplicate and one-edit families are grouped before selection.
Examples are held-out evaluation/development material, never model training,
never bundled in the APK. They are dictionary usage examples, not private
messages or measured Hong Kong chat/typing frequencies.
